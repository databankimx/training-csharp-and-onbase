# DataBank IMX: AI Acceptable Use Policy

Version 1.7 - Updated October 2024

This policy defines guidelines for the responsible, ethical, and productive use of AI systems at DataBank. It applies to all employees, contractors, temporary workers, and third-party partners who interact with AI systems, whether developed internally or sourced from external vendors.

The full policy is available on the employee portal at workforcenow.adp.com. For questions, contact the Information Security team via the helpdesk at https://helpdesk.databankimx.com (Mon-Fri 7am-5pm, 1-267-817-2881). For emergency IT assistance: 1-866-363-6813.

---

## General Guidelines

### AI as a support tool, not a decision maker

AI can assist with tasks, but human judgment must always prevail. AI outputs must be reviewed before acting on them, especially for critical decisions involving document processing, customer communications, or business operations.

### Respect for privacy and confidentiality (zero-trust data handling)

All data must be treated as **CONFIDENTIAL by default**, whether internal or external, unless explicitly proven otherwise. Do not enter customer data, passwords, or confidential project details into any public or unapproved AI tool.

Any data you input into an AI system must comply with the same confidentiality criteria, sensitivity, and access restrictions that apply to classified information handling.

*Example: If working on a customer report, do not enter customer names, account details, or sensitive project information into an AI tool unless it is an approved, secure tool. Use placeholders or general descriptions instead, or get approval from IT for an authorized tool.*

### Bias and fairness

AI outputs must be critically assessed for fairness, especially in marketing, customer communications, hiring, and other decision-making workflows. AI systems must not be used to discriminate against individuals or groups based on race, gender, age, religion, or any other protected characteristic.

### Transparency

If AI tools are used to directly influence decisions or communications that impact customers or partners, ensure the content is reviewed and accurate before sharing. There is no need to disclose the use of AI for internal tasks such as grammar checks or tone adjustments, as long as the final output is human-reviewed.

## Security and Data Privacy

Your responsibility for protecting sensitive company and customer data does not change when using AI tools.

### Secure channels only

Use only AI tools approved by the IT department. All interactions with AI systems must align with DataBank's existing IT policies. Do not use personal accounts to interact with AI tools for company-related work.

### Classified content by default (zero-trust approach)

All information must be considered CONFIDENTIAL unless explicitly cleared by the Information Security team and determined to be non-sensitive. Do not upload or share personal, customer, or proprietary data into any AI system that has not been cleared.

### Compliance

All AI systems must comply with applicable data protection regulations, including HIPAA, GDPR, and applicable federal and state laws, when handling personal or customer data.

### Vendor risk assessment

Do not use any new AI tool or service until it has been approved by IT. If you identify a new AI tool that could improve productivity, submit a request for review by opening a helpdesk ticket. If working with a new AI vendor, open a helpdesk ticket for a formal vendor risk assessment before proceeding.

## Ethical Use of AI

- **Non-discriminatory practices:** AI must not be used to discriminate against individuals or groups based on any protected characteristic.
- **Intellectual property:** Do not use proprietary algorithms, datasets, or software without proper authorization or licensing.
- **Documentation:** For any AI system development or training, document processes including data sources, training processes, and algorithm selection. Submit documentation via a helpdesk ticket to maintain a formal audit trail.
- **Avoid manipulation:** AI must not be used to mislead or manipulate individuals or customers. Do not deploy AI-generated content that could create confusion or spread false information.
- **Human oversight in critical situations:** In workflow automation, financial decisions, or customer service contexts, human oversight is required. AI assists; humans decide.

---

## Practical Dos and Don'ts

**Do:**
- Use AI tools to enhance productivity - automating routine tasks, generating first drafts for review.
- Verify AI outputs before acting on them, especially in customer-facing or critical business scenarios.
- Submit new AI tools for approval via a helpdesk ticket before use.
- Report any issues or incidents involving AI to IT and Information Security immediately by opening a helpdesk ticket.

**Don't:**
- Upload sensitive business or customer data into any unapproved AI system.
- Assume data is non-sensitive by default - treat all data as classified until proven otherwise.
- Rely solely on AI for decisions involving legal, financial, or operational issues without human oversight.
- Bypass IT security policies when experimenting with AI tools.
- Use company resources for personal or non-work-related AI projects.

---

## Incident Reporting and Accountability

If you encounter any issues - including incorrect or biased AI outputs, security breaches, or policy violations - report them immediately to IT and Information Security by opening a helpdesk ticket or calling IT support.

In the event of a suspected or confirmed breach, report to your supervisor, the CIO, CTO, or the Human Resources Department. AI-related incidents will be formally investigated and employees are expected to cooperate fully.

**Consequences:** Violations of this policy may result in disciplinary action up to and including termination of employment, and could lead to legal consequences where applicable.

---

## Approved AI Tools and Innovation

Employees may test new AI tools and innovations in a secure sandbox environment approved by the IT team. New tools must be submitted for review via the helpdesk before use in any work context.

DataBank encourages the use of AI to streamline processes and innovate - within these guidelines. Stay curious, but remain vigilant, and reach out to the IT team for guidance whenever in doubt.
