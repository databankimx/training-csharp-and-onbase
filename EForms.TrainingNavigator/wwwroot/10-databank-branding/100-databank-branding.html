Logos

Use the primary logo whenever possible. Mono, simplified, and mark-only assets are supporting options for contrast, size, repetition, or specific contexts.

Primary Logo

  • Use for internal and external media whenever possible.
  • Appropriate for formal documents, contracts, letterhead, and email signatures.
  • Includes the icon, DataBank name, and Kyocera Group subtext.

Alternate Logo Use

  • Use mono logos when the primary mark lacks contrast or the piece needs visual restraint.
  • Use simplified logos when the primary logo would be too small or repeated often.
  • Use the logo mark only for marketing additions or DataBank-owned social media.

Background Fills

Primary and accent color cards with values formatted to match the brand color references.

Color Use Ratios

  • Formal documents: keep the dominant primary background near 70%, with remaining supporting colors around 15% each.
  • All other content: keep the dominant background near 60%, with supporting color areas around 15%, 15%, and 10%.
  • Use accent colors sparingly for icons, graphics, and supplemental visuals rather than main backgrounds or typography.
