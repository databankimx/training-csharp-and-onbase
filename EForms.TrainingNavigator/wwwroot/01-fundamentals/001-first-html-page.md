# Your First HTML Page

This lesson puts two of the most common HTML elements on the page together for the first time: a heading and a paragraph.

## `<h1>...</h1>`

Headings introduce a section of content. `<h1>` is the largest and most important heading level - think of it as the title of the page or of the main content on it. Browsers render it in a large, bold font by default, but its real purpose is semantic (telling the browser, screen readers, and search engines "this is the main heading here"), not purely visual - see the next lesson for the full range of heading levels.

## `<p>...</p>`

The `<p>` tag defines a paragraph of text. Browsers automatically add space before and after each paragraph, so you don't need to add manual line breaks (`<br>`) between them.

A page is normally built from a mix of headings (to organize content into sections) and paragraphs (to hold the actual text), plus the many other elements covered in the lessons that follow.
