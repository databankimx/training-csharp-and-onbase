# Arrow Entities

A reference table of arrow symbols in every direction and style - simple (`&larr;`, `&rarr;`, `&uarr;`, `&darr;`), double-line (`&lArr;`, `&rArr;`), diagonal, curved, and paired/bidirectional arrows.

Commonly used for navigation cues (next/previous, expand/collapse indicators), mathematical implication (`&rArr;` for "implies"), and general directional icons where an image isn't warranted.
