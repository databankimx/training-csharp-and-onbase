# General HTML Entities

A reference table of other commonly-needed entities beyond the basics: punctuation (`&mdash;`, `&hellip;`, curly/angled quotes), invisible spacing/formatting characters (`&nbsp;`-like spaces of various widths, zero-width joiners, direction markers), and a handful of symbols (card suits, angle brackets, section/paragraph marks).

The "Character" column shows some entries bracketed (e.g. `[&shy;]`) - these are invisible or zero-width characters that render as nothing on their own; the brackets are just this table's own way of showing where the character sits.
