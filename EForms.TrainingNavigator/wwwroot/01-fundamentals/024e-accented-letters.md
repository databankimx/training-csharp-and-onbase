# Accented Letters, Ligatures, and IPA Characters

Three related reference tables:

- **Accented Letters** - pre-composed letters with a diacritical mark built in as a single character (`&eacute;` → é), covering the standalone diacritic marks themselves plus every common accented Latin letter, upper and lower case.
- **Ligatures** - two letters combined into one glyph (`&aelig;` → æ), used in several languages' standard spelling.
- **IPA Characters** - letters used in the International Phonetic Alphabet and a few Northern European languages (`&eth;`/`&thorn;` → ð/þ, from Old English and Icelandic).

Where a pre-composed accented letter exists here, prefer it over building the same letter from a base character plus a combining diacritic (see the Combining Diacritics lesson) - it's simpler and avoids that lesson's own validator warning.
