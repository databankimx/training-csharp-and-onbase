Alpha (Opacity)

Each color format also supports a fourth value for alpha (opacity), from 0 (fully transparent) to 1 (fully opaque), or as an extra two hex digits (00 to FF):

rgba()

rgba(255, 99, 71, 0.0)
rgba(255, 99, 71, 0.2)
rgba(255, 99, 71, 0.4)
rgba(255, 99, 71, 0.6)
rgba(255, 99, 71, 0.8)
rgba(255, 99, 71, 1.0)
