Using Responsive Design in HTML

Resize the browser window to see how each of these three techniques responds differently.

Responsive Image (Width)

Always renders at 50% of its container's width - scales both up and down.

Responsive Image (Max-Width)

Never exceeds 75% of its container's width, but won't scale up past its natural size.

Non-Responsive Image

No width constraint at all - renders at its natural pixel size regardless of viewport.
